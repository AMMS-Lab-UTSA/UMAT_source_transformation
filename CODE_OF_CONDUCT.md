# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds a good research-software community:

- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including on scientific
  correctness. A reviewer who shows that a published number is wrong is doing
  the project a service.
- Being explicit about uncertainty. "I could not reproduce this" is a welcome
  contribution.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the correctness of the
  results.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.
- Knowingly misrepresenting results, provenance, or the state of verification.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers through the repository's
GitHub issue tracker for public matters, or privately via GitHub to a maintainer
listed in [`AUTHORS.md`](AUTHORS.md) for anything sensitive. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will
explain their reasons when doing so.
