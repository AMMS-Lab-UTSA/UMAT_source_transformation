### Corpus tooling reuse decision (framework_repos/umat-oti/tools/corpus) + automatic contract-synthesis design for the active SoftwareX repo

The legacy `tools/corpus/` bundle and the active repo's `src/umat_oti/corpus/` solve overlapping halves of the same problem and neither is complete. The legacy tool is strong at *acquisition* (repo-search discovery, shallow-clone harvest that dodges API limits, byte-exact dedup, resumable index) and, crucially, at *locating the DDSDDE tangent block* — its `corpus_batch._fallback_ranges` is the one algorithm with a measured effect (no_ddsdde_region 66→17, transform success 35→65 on 207 UMATs, per its REPORT.md). Its architecture should be discarded wholesale: sys.path hacks, no license *categorisation*, no immutable pin, and a summary figure whose numbers are Python literals. The active repo has the better skeleton — a canonical 10-stage vocabulary, a 16-entry failure taxonomy, license classification, normalized-form hashing, snapshot cache — but its contract synthesis is a stub: `corpus/cli.py::_process_one` writes `ntens: 4, promote: [], replace: []` and then skips `merge_completed_anchors_into_config` entirely, unlike `services/transformation.py:116` and unlike the legacy batch runner. That stub is the direct cause of 31 of the 119 failures in `corpus_round_metrics.json` (22 `confirmed_transformation_defect` + 8 `dimension_inference_failure` + 1 `unsupported_fortran_construct`), and mislabelling contract-synthesis failures as confirmed engine defects is a paper-integrity risk. ### Generic extraction of internal constitutive Jacobians (FJAC, DETDG, GDIA, ANP1P, BNP1P, CEVPI) in UMAT-OTI

All six symbols live only in the ICP UMAT family (10 files, 21 distinct symbol-model pairs); nine of the ten models share one textbook scalar Newton solve on the consistency parameter GAM_PAR, and the triple (iteration variable, residual, hand-coded Jacobian) = (GAM_PAR, FGAM, FJAC) is recoverable at exact line numbers by a purely syntactic scan for `X = X - A/B`, with no model names and no symbol whitelist — I ran that scan and it hit 9/9. FJAC is dFGAM/dGAM_PAR; ANP1P and BNP1P are dANP1/dGAM_PAR and dBNP1/dGAM_PAR; DETDG is dPHIINV/dGAM_PAR (Perzyna overstress); GDIA is d(DIAG)/dGAM_PAR; CEVPI is the odd one out — HIN has no Newton update at all, and CEVPI is KINVER(DEE + dt*XKAPPA*G1), the inverse of a local viscoplastic operator. Today's contracts say nothing about any of these as derivative targets: they appear only in `variables.{promote,constant,real}` type-classification lists, and DETDG is even classified `constant` in NKH/VPDCL/VPDCO while FJAC/ANP1P/BNP1P are `promote`, which truncates the chain that feeds FJAC. `derivative_request.py` can already express KIND_LOCAL_JACOBIAN but nothing consumes it; the real working mechanism is `extra_jacobian_contracts`, which already reseeds at a loop anchor with `REAL(x_OTI) + OTI_E{slot}`, extracts with `GETIM`, blanks the hand-coded assignment, and auto-bumps nbases 4 to 5 — it has simply never been pointed at a local Jacobian. The generic algorithm therefore needs no new codegen primitives, only a discovery rule and a closure rule that replaces the hand-written `reseed_prelude` Fortran escape hatch; while reading the sources I also confirmed numerically that ANP1P (UMAT_NKH_1.02.for:195/226) and GDIA(3,3) (UMAT_VPDCO.for:539, UMAT_VPDCL_R.for:541) each drop one factor of (1-D), so the hand-coded Jacobians are wrong by 1/(1-D) whenever damage is active.
